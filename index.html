Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To make disciples of Jesus Christ who love God, love others, and serve the world through the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our Vision

To be a thriving community of believers who transform lives and communities through authentic relationships and faithful service.

Our Core Values

Love

We love God with all our heart and love our neighbors as ourselves.

Truth

We believe in the authority of Scripture and live by God's truth.

Service

We serve others with humility and use our gifts for God's glory.
